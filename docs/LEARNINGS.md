## Gemini API

### The 503 Error 

**What I learned**
The 503 error meant that Gemini couldn't process your request because the model was experiencing high demand. I discovered that it could also be a caused by my response schema structure and not a temporary high demand from the Gemini API. In short, the changing the structure of the schema resolved the issue.

UPDATE: It can still be that the model really just experiencing high demand so just change to a less popular model

**Why it matters**
Understanding how schemas affect the response that AI generates helps prevent future errors
